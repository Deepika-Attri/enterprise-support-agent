# NovaCart Employee Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives 18 paid leaves per year, credited on January 1st.
Unused leaves can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days.
Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 2 weeks.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal, except in emergencies.
