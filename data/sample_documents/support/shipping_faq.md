# NovaCart Shipping FAQ

Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and is free for orders above 999 rupees.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 149 rupees.
Orders placed before 2 PM on a working day are dispatched the same day.
We currently ship to all major cities in India. International shipping is not available.
Tracking details are sent by SMS and email once the order is dispatched.
